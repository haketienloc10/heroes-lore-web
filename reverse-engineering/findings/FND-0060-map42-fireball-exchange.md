# FND-0060 — Map42 level-8 combat exchange

## Question

Which class-6 attacks damage a Map42 type-2 enemy from the level-8 campaign checkpoint, and what player-health cost is observed?

## Reference and method

- Pinned reference is Heroes Lore: Wind of Soltia v2.1.2, JAR SHA-256 `7eb032297967f5d8382cc4f1ab5c3016459e01b235b42a3b88c6a8bfe46c5f61`. The original JAR was not opened for M693–M705.
- Chromium used the disposable `/tmp/heroes-lore-campaign-M675` profile and the existing visible Map42 Load Game checkpoint. The game was controlled with ordinary keyboard input over CDP. State observations were read-only through the runtime debug observer.
- M698 cross-checks checked-in decompiled `an.java` with the browser implementation and generated enemy/equipment/commerce catalogs. This is static source/model analysis, not a J2ME runtime comparison.
- No browser storage was read or written, no gameplay state was seeded, and no Save Game action was used. The checkpoint therefore remains unchanged.

This matches checked-in decompiled `an.n()` and the web `calculateEquipmentStats()` formula: weapon value 10 plus Power 8 scaled by `4/5` gives power 16. `an.m15a()` and the web combo path then produce light power 16 and heavy power 26–28 after signed random variation. Variant-0 enemy record 2 has defense 59, so both attacks resolve to zero damage after defense. The ordinary evasion threshold is `min(50, 52 - 3 + 10) = 50`; a physical attack may also be evaded. The unchanged target HP in M695/M696 therefore does not by itself indicate an attack-targeting defect.

This is a catalog/formula projection; it does not show that the campaign can earn or spend those amounts before the encounters.

## Conclusion and limits

- This live browser probe shows Fireball is direction-dependent in this encounter: a target that moved to the side was missed, while an aligned target took 152 damage at four-figure HP. The aligned cast window also cost the player 153 HP. M698 cross-checks the web attack-stat formula against the checked-in decompiled source and explains why the loaded starter build's physical attacks do no damage: even heavy power 26–28 is below defense 59. It also gives the ordinary physical hit path a 50% capped evasion threshold.
- This does not prove the encounter or campaign is unwinnable. The checkpoint has no spare attribute points, and the tested route has not established a weapon upgrade that raises power above defense. M699 died before a cast; M700's lower-corridor retreat is caught between two enemies; M702 instead exits Map42 through marker 3 after an east-facing Fireball action and reaches Map15 at 242 HP. M704 does not establish a safe repeat-cast interval, and M705 finds no idle HP recovery during the Map14 entry cutscene before reaching Map15 at 76 HP. No Map42 enemy was defeated, and the three linked Map15 enemies remain alive. Healing between casts, a full encounter, and J2ME timing/visual comparisons remain open.
