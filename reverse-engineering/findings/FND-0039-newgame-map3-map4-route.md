## Opcode 7 arrival facing (M167)

The second byte in an opcode-7 transfer is the destination-facing index, not a region ID. In the pinned JAR, `ag.i()` passes that byte to `m.b(mapId, directionIndex, tileX, tileY)`; `m.f()` then calls `n.b(directionIndex + 1)`, and `n.b(byte)` stores the resulting hero direction. Thus script indices `0–3` select source direction codes `1–4` (North, South, West, East). The web interpreter preserves this as `entryDirectionIndex` and uses the corresponding facing when creating the destination actor; older `regionId` naming was incorrect.

This correction is based on local JADX decompilation and `javap -c -p ag m n` against the pinned `heroes-lore-v2.1.2.jar` (SHA-256 recorded above). REA was used to open and hash-check the archive, not to query bytecode. Focused event/actor tests cover the mapping and bounds. M167 produced no new browser capture or J2ME comparison.
