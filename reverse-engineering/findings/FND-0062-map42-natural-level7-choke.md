# FND-0062: Natural New Game level-up and the Map42 lower choke

## Finding

A visible, unseeded New Game route can gain level 7 by repeatedly defeating naturally respawning map-27 enemy ID 24 entities. This increases the Ronin's HP ceiling to 360 after allocating the earned points to HP. In M782, enemy ID 2/order 5 occupied the narrow Map42 lower corridor at `(7,24)` and `(8,24)`: the route took 304 HP from it with Fireball, then died at `(8,23)` while trying to move south.

Two later natural New Game routes reached level 8 and the Map42 lower switch with 408 maximum HP. M787 made no Fireball cast because its temporary runner omitted enemy `state` from its snapshot while requiring that field in the casting loop. M789 made one actual Fireball cast, reducing the same enemy from 1905 to 1753 HP, but died before a second cast. Neither attempt cleared the corridor.

M794 reached the same switch on another level-8 New Game and landed five Fireball casts, reducing that enemy from 1905 to 917 HP while returning to the switch at full HP after each cast. The enemy then pursued the player to the switch, where the hero died during the recharge and Heal attempt. This establishes a longer live exchange, but the switch is not a safe indefinite recovery point.

M795 followed a live occupancy-aware route from the switch back to the lower exit and reached Map41 at 408/408 HP without defeating the Map42 enemy. It later died on the Map41 return to the upper portal. The observed Map42 passage therefore does not require defeating that enemy in every live arrangement.

M796 repeated the lower-exit passage, completed the recorded M517 Map41 return at 408 HP, and naturally reached Map15 at 408/408 HP. Two Fireball casts reduced boss 39 from 14,430 to 13,974 HP. The runner stopped while looking for a third firing window; the boss was not defeated.

This is a controlled browser route observation. It does not prove the corridor has no other route, that the original J2ME game behaves identically, or that the Map42 encounter is impossible to defeat.

A Fireball cast from `(6,24)` reduces Map42 enemy ID 2/order 5 from 1905 to 1601 HP and leaves the hero at `(5,24)`. The route then reaches `(8,23)` and repeatedly fails to move south while the enemy occupies `(7,24)` and `(8,24)`; the hero dies at that cell.
- The post-death read records Map42 enemy order 5 alive at `(7,24)` with 1601 HP, occupancy on `(7,24)` and `(8,24)`, and the player at `(8,23)` with 0 HP. The visible base collision grid has walls to the north and east of `(8,23)`; the cell south is occupied by the enemy. The attempted route did not defeat or move the enemy out of the choke.
- M787 reached level 8 after seven initial kills and 46 additional natural respawn kills, assigned the nine remaining attribute points to HP, and reached the Map42 lower switch at 408/408 HP. Its continuation trace records zero Fireball casts with enemy order 5 still at 1905 HP. The temporary runner filtered for living enemies but omitted `state` in its projected snapshot; its loop then required `blocker.state === 'alive'`. This is a runner failure, so it does not establish a Fireball failure in the game.
- M789 reached level 8 after seven initial kills and 53 additional natural respawn kills, assigned nine points to HP, and reached the same switch at 408/408 HP. Its lane was planned at distance three, but the enemy advanced to `(8,25)` while the hero approached `(8,24)`. The cast therefore began at distance one. Two observed damage steps of 76 reduced enemy HP from 1905 to 1753; the hero fell from 408 to 246 HP during the cast and retreat, and subsequently died while attempting recovery near the enemy. The stale lane and interrupted exchange do not demonstrate a full uninterrupted cast or sustained encounter viability.
- M790 reached Map1 at level 8 with 274 HP, then died while the runner waited at the entry tile for Heal to recharge. M792 skipped that wait, reached Map51 with 303 HP, and died at `(8,33)` after the continuation runner attempted melee against a strong enemy blocking the route. These are separate route and runner limitations; neither is another Map42 combat attempt.

Five casts from live-selected lanes dealt 152, 304, 152, 76 and 304 damage respectively; after each cast the hero returned to `(4,15)` with 408 HP. The hero fell to 237 HP at the switch; a Heal attempt under pressure was followed by death at `(4,15)`, with the target still alive at 917 HP. - A read-only analysis of M794's post-death Map42 collision grid and the recorded occupancy at the switch found a 55-step path from `(4,15)` back to the lower exit `(37,29)` before the first cast; the corresponding search to the upper marker `(8,2)` found no path. The same lower-exit search found paths in all five post-cast switch snapshots. This is static connectivity at each snapshot, not a live traversal: enemies can move and close a path while the player follows it. The M794 runner checked the upper marker before entering its Fireball loop and did not attempt the lower exit from the switch.
- M795 began a fresh visible New Game, naturally reached level 8 and 408 maximum HP, activated flag 51 at `(4,15)`, and found a 61-step live path to the lower exit. It followed live occupancy replanning to `(37,29)` and transferred to Map41 `(2,29)` with 408 HP; no Fireball was cast at the Map42 blocker. On the subsequent Map41 lower-to-upper route it lost HP near `(26,18)`, attempted Heal under pressure, and died there before reaching the upper portal. This verifies the lower-exit passage for that run, not the subsequent return or the enemy's defeat.
- M796 found a 67-step live path from the flag-51 switch to the Map42 lower exit and reached Map41 with 408 HP. It followed the 102-step M517 route to Map41 `(2,3)` at 408 HP, returned to Map42's upper entrance, and reached Map15 `(7,10)` at 408 HP. Its separately recorded Map27 intervention used ordinary keyboard inputs to remove a blocking enemy. ## Limits and next evidence

No original JAR contents, browser storage, or local save files were inspected. No in-game Save/Load or gameplay-state seed was used. The traces use synthetic Chrome DevTools keyboard input. M796 reaches Map15 but does not defeat its boss or reach the ending. A repeatable boss strategy and J2ME comparisons remain necessary before claiming campaign completion or parity.


## M800: blocked live exit and static-path fallback

M800 starts a visible New Game. Its first runner stops at the already-open Reah rescue dialogue; a separately recorded sequence of 73 ordinary Enter inputs completes the rescue and recovery, and the same live page is resumed. After reaching level 8, the continuation reaches the Map42 flag-51 lower switch `(4,15)` at 408/408 HP. The live planner initially reports a 79-step path to the lower exit. During execution the path disappears; the static-collision fallback advances to `(8,23)` and repeatedly attempts Down without moving. Recorded HP changes 408→231, then after recovery 257→80, and the final snapshot reports 0 HP at `(8,23)` with enemy 2/order 5 at `(7,24)` and 1,905 HP. The final occupancy set includes both `(7,24)` and `(8,24)`, the cell immediately below the hero. In the generated base collision grid, Up and Right from `(8,23)` are walls and Left `(7,23)` is ordinary floor; the final occupancy set does not mark that left cell occupied. These snapshots do not prove occupancy at every earlier blocked input or that a retreat would have survived. The exact fatal attacker is not established. No Map15 entry is observed. This shows a failure of this live route/fallback in the encounter, not general Map42 unreachability: M795, M796 and M798 separately reached the lower exit.

The initial and resumed runners, intervention inputs, and continuation are preserved separately; the resumed parent route does not duplicate the intervention's Enter inputs. The continuation records zero page exceptions, console errors, failed requests and bad responses. No in-game Save/Load, browser-storage inspection, local-save inspection, original-JAR inspection, or gameplay-state seed was used. After the Map42 lower loop and Map41 return, the hero enters the upper Map42 route with 471 HP and later reaches `(35,3)`. The continuation records all 40 items being used at that tile, followed by blocked movement and 0 HP; no Map15 or Map82 arrival occurs. The final enemy snapshot includes enemy 2/order 0 at `(35,4)`.
