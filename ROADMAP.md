# Heroes Lore: Wind of Soltia v2.1.2 — Port roadmap

**Mission:** Build a faithful, complete browser port of the pinned J2ME game. The goal remains the whole game; an emulator setup, extracted assets, research report, or vertical slice alone does not satisfy it.

**Reference JAR:** SHA-256 `7eb032297967f5d8382cc4f1ab5c3016459e01b235b42a3b88c6a8bfe46c5f61` (verified locally on 2026-10-23). The JAR and copyrighted assets remain local and ignored by Git.
